Closing the Rural Digital Divide in Scotland

Understanding rural digital inequality and developing community-led solutions.

Skywave RuralNet works to understand and reduce digital exclusion in rural communities through research, community engagement and practical digital inclusion solutions.

Why We Exist

Digital exclusion is not only about whether an internet connection exists. People can also be excluded because they lack a suitable device, digital skills, confidence or access to practical support.

Connectivity

Rural households and communities can experience limited, unreliable or inadequate broadband access.

Devices

Having a connection is not enough if people do not have access to a suitable device.

Digital Skills

People may need skills, confidence and guidance to use digital services effectively.

Digital Support

Some people need ongoing practical support to overcome digital barriers.

About Us

Skywave RuralNet SCIO is a Scottish Charitable Incorporated Organisation dedicated to reducing digital exclusion across rural Scotland. We believe that digital inclusion is about more than having an internet connection. People also need access to suitable devices, digital skills, confidence and practical support to benefit from the digital world. Our work combines research, community engagement and the development of practical digital inclusion solutions. We use data and postcode-level mapping to understand where inequalities exist and work with communities and partners to identify what support is needed. Our approach considers four connected areas: connectivity, digital devices, digital skills and digital support. By understanding the needs of individual communities, we aim to develop solutions that are relevant, sustainable and capable of being adapted across rural Scotland.

Our Mission

To reduce digital exclusion in rural Scotland by developing evidence-based, community-led solutions that improve connectivity, increase digital confidence, and create equal opportunities for everyone to participate in the digital world.

Our Vision

A rural Scotland where every community has access to reliable digital connectivity, the skills and confidence to use it effectively, and equal opportunities to thrive socially, economically, and culturally in an increasingly digital society.

Research & Evidence

Good digital inclusion starts with understanding where the barriers are and what communities actually need. Skywave RuralNet uses postcode-level data, spatial analysis and community engagement to build a more detailed picture of rural digital inequality.

Our 2026 Pilot Research

Our current pilot research examines postcode districts IV26, IV27 and IV28, helping us understand how broadband access varies between communities.

Key Finding

Broadband inequality is spatially clustered and cannot be understood using simple "served vs underserved" classification.

IV26

Large number of premises experience intermediate broadband connectivity requiring targetted improvement

IV27

Significant Variation exists between well served and under served communities

IV28

The highest level of broadband deprivation identified in our study
